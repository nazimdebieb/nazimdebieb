# Saga-map puzzle economies: Candy Crush Saga (King), Royal Match (Dream Games), Toon Blast (Peak)

Research date: 2026-09-30. App Store IAP lists were fetched live on that date (FR and US storefronts). Official help-center pages show "Last updated N days" counted from 2026-09-30. Fan wikis (Fandom) are community-written and marked as such. Where a number comes only from a search-engine snippet, a reseller, or a user review, it is flagged.

Currency model, in one line:
- **Candy Crush Saga** has only one currency, **Gold Bars**, and it is a premium one. Players earn no gold for ordinary level wins; gold comes from purchases and a few events.
- **Royal Match** has one currency, **Coins**, earned on every level and spent on moves, lives and boosters.
- **Toon Blast** has one currency, **Coins**. The official FAQ does not list level wins as a coin source.

## 1. Soft currency earned per level won (normal / hard / super hard) and end-of-area/episode rewards

### Takeaway
Royal Match is the only one of the three that pays coins on every level: 10, 20 or 30 coins by difficulty, plus 1 coin per tile cleared by leftover moves, plus area chests and bonus levels. Candy Crush pays no gold per level. Toon Blast's official help center does not list level wins as a coin source; a user review describes a paid "piggy bank" that stores 50 coins per level.

### Cited Findings
**Royal Match**
- Official list of free coin sources: "Opening the Area Chest; Completing Bonus Levels; Beating Normal, Hard, Super Hard and Extremely Hard levels; With the remaining moves at the end of a beaten level; From events…" (help page last updated about 8 days before 2026-09-30). — [Dream Games Help: How to get free coins?](https://dreamgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/3-royal-match/faq/10-how-to-get-free-coins/)
- Base coin reward per level by difficulty: **10, 20 or 30 coins**. Letting the end-of-level celebration play (leftover moves become boosters that clear tiles) "can multiply coin income from a single level by 3 to 10 times". The article is undated; it is a secondary industry blog. — [Gamigion: Royal Match has a clever trick for creating coin demand](https://www.gamigion.com/royal-match-has-a-clever-trick-for-creating-coin-demand/)
- Each leftover move becomes a random booster that fires at level end, and each tile it removes (including cascades) gives 1 free coin. Written by a player, January 2024. — [Old Cynic: Royal Match tips](https://oldcynic.com/royal-match-tips-and-tricks-cheats-for-new-players)
- Bonus Level: "your goal is to collect as many coins as possible till you run out of available moves" (official). — [Dream Games Help: What is Bonus Level?](https://dreamgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/3-royal-match/faq/13-what-is-bonus-level/)
- Bonus-level payout of **300–700 coins**, described as "generous… compared to the standard post-level coin drop". **This is 2021 soft-launch data.** — [Deconstructor of Fun, Mar 2021](https://www.deconstructoroffun.com/blog/2021/3/21/royal-match-the-new-king-from-turkey)
- A search snippet attributed to a Medium economy article gives "bonus levels occur every 10 levels … 300–450 coins". I could not open the page (HTTP 403), so this is unverified. — [Medium: The intricate economy of Royal Match](https://medium.com/@madhav_g/behind-the-scenes-the-intricate-economy-of-royal-match-7c191b760870)
- King's Nightmare mini-episodes (the ad-style puzzles) pay **50 coins** when completed; failing costs no life. — [Royal Match Fandom: King's Nightmare](https://royalmatch.fandom.com/wiki/King%27s_Nightmare); confirmed by [Udonis, Sep 2025](https://www.blog.udonis.co/mobile-marketing/mobile-games/royal-match-analysis)
- Area chest: finishing all tasks in an area opens a chest with coins, boosters and cards. No official amount is given. — [Dream Games Help: coins](https://dreamgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/3-royal-match/faq/21-how-can-i-obtain-and-use-coins/); [Dream Games Help: unlock new area](https://dreamgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/3-royal-match/faq/17-how-can-i-unlock-a-new-area/)
- Scale of what free play can build up: a free-to-play player reports **577,226 coins** saved by level ~11,203 (Jan 2026). Another commenter reports 53,360 coins earned on a free account (2024). — [Old Cynic: 550K coins account reveal](https://oldcynic.com/royal-match-550k-free-coins-boosters); [Old Cynic tips (comments)](https://oldcynic.com/royal-match-tips-and-tricks-cheats-for-new-players)

**Candy Crush Saga**
- There is "no permanent common currency". Gold is the premium currency, and only "meagre amounts" are earned through gameplay events. — [Candy Crush Fandom: Gold](https://candycrush.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_Bar)
- Candy Crush "does not have a non-premium currency that can be constantly obtained through regular gameplay, although very meager amounts of gold can be obtained." — [Candy Crush Fandom: Boosters](https://candycrush.fandom.com/wiki/Boosters)
- Historical: in September 2013 the game gave 50 free gold bars for completing the first 10 levels. — [Candy Crush Fandom: Gold](https://candycrush.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_Bar)
- Episode completion pays gold only through the **Episode Race** event (details in section 3). — [Candy Crush Fandom: Episode Race](https://candycrush.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_Race)

**Toon Blast**
- Official free coin sources: "Helping teammates fill their life requests; Connecting your Facebook account; Joining a team for the first time; Revealing a Coin card in the Daily Bonus". Level wins are not listed (page last updated about 384 days before 2026-09-30). — [Peak Help: What are coins](https://peakgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/4-toon-blast/faq/49-what-are-coins-and-how-do-i-get-them/); [Peak Help: How can I earn free coins?](https://peakgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/4-toon-blast/faq/16-how-can-i-earn-free-coins/)
- Sending 1 life to a teammate gives 1 coin. — [Toon Blast Fandom: Coins](https://toonblast.fandom.com/wiki/Coins)
- A Google Play FR user review (undated, fetched 2026-09-30) mentions a "tirelire" (piggy bank) that stores **50 coins per level passed**, which the player must buy for **€3.29** to unlock. — [Google Play FR: Toon Blast reviews](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=net.peakgames.toonblast&hl=fr&gl=FR)
- Episode structure: the first 10 episodes have 20 levels each, episodes 11–13 have 40, episode 14 has 30, and from episode 15 onward each has 50. I found no source for an episode-completion coin reward. — [Toon Blast Fandom: Episodes](https://toonblast.fandom.com/wiki/Episodes)

### Inferences
- In Royal Match, a normal win pays about 10 coins before the move bonus, while a +5 moves purchase costs 900 coins. That is about 90 normal wins, or 30 super-hard wins, of base reward for one continue. The leftover-move bonus and the 300–700-coin bonus levels do most of the work of keeping continues affordable.
- For Pop Archer, the Royal Match pattern is: small fixed pay by difficulty, a skill bonus from unused resources, and occasional bonus stages with large payouts.

### Gaps
- No official per-level coin amounts from Dream Games. The 10/20/30 figure comes from one secondary blog.
- No verified area-chest coin amount for Royal Match.
- No official confirmation or date for Toon Blast's per-level piggy bank.
- No verified Candy Crush gold per episode other than Episode Race.

## 2. Daily rewards (login gift, daily wheel / spin, streak scaling)

### Takeaway
Candy Crush has the richest daily system: a daily booster wheel, a 30-day Daily Treat Machine that charges up, and Daily Stamps. Toon Blast has a Daily Bonus card flip (a coin card is 50 coins) and a daily tap mini-game. For Royal Match I found no documented daily login gift; retention there runs on win streaks and events.

### Cited Findings
**Candy Crush Saga**
- **Daily Booster Wheel**: one free spin per day for one booster. Segments: Jelly Fish, Colour Bomb, Lollipop Hammer, Party Popper (or Free Switch), Coconut Wheel, 15-minute UFO pass (or Lucky Candy), Striped+Wrapped, and a Jackpot of 3 of each booster (21 boosters). Jackpot odds were estimated at about 1:450 before June 2020 and about 1:15 since.
  - Since 27 May 2026, extra spins use **Spin Tokens** from events, and the wheel unlocks at level 89 for new players.
  - An older paid "second wheel" cost 6 gold per spin (mobile weekend offer) and could pay up to 500 gold.
  - Source: [Candy Crush Fandom: Daily Booster Wheel](https://candycrush.fandom.com/wiki/Daily_Booster_Wheel)
- **Daily Treat Machine** (released 12 July 2021, unlocks at level 67) gives one free treat per day. It reaches full power after 30 consecutive days. If a player at max misses a day, it drops back to day 25. The wiki gives no per-day contents. — [Candy Crush Fandom: Daily Treat Machine](https://candycrush.fandom.com/wiki/Daily_Treat_Machine)
- **Daily Stamps** (released 9 June 2025): 1 stamp per day for passing a level, and 6 stamps earn a booster reward. — [Candy Crush Fandom: Daily Stamps](https://candycrush.fandom.com/wiki/Daily_Stamps)

**Toon Blast**
- A Daily Bonus coin card gives **50 coins**. — [Toon Blast Fandom: Coins](https://toonblast.fandom.com/wiki/Coins)
- The Daily Bonus can also give boosters (Hammer, Boxing Glove, Anvil, Dice) and special items (Rocket, Bomb, Disco Ball). — [Peak Help: What are boosters?](https://peakgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/4-toon-blast/faq/8-what-are-boosters/); [Peak Help: special items](https://peakgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/4-toon-blast/faq/7-what-are-the-special-items/)
- The Daily Bonus can give a life, added to the gift box even above 10 lives. — [Toon Blast Fandom: Lives](https://toonblast.fandom.com/wiki/Lives)
- **Surprise Calendar** mini-game: once per day, tap falling gifts for coins, while TNT deducts coins. Playing 6 days gives a surprise gift. Requires level 35. — [Peak Help: Surprise Calendar](https://peakgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/4-toon-blast/faq/555-what-is-surprise-calendar/)

**Royal Match**
- The official free-coin list (sources: [Dream Games Help](https://dreamgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/3-royal-match/faq/10-how-to-get-free-coins/)) contains no daily login reward.
- Streak mechanic: **Butler's Gift**, from level 32. Consecutive wins without failing give free pre-level boosters:
  - Stage 1: 1 Rocket + 1 Propeller
  - Stage 2: adds 1 TNT
  - Stage 3 (max, kept until a loss): Rocket + Propeller + TNT + Light Ball
  - A loss resets it.
  - Source: [Dream Games Help: Butler's Gift](https://dreamgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/3-royal-match/faq/78-what-is-butler-s-gift/)

### Inferences
- None of the three uses a classic "Day 1…Day 7 coin calendar" that I could document. Candy Crush's Treat Machine (30-day charge, partial reset) and Toon Blast's 6-day calendar are the closest. Royal Match relies on win streaks instead of login streaks.

### Gaps
- Exact Daily Treat Machine contents per day.
- Toon Blast Daily Bonus card odds and full prize table.
- Whether Royal Match has any daily gift in 2026. None was found in the help center.

## 3. Events and challenges (streak events, team events, chests): typical rewards

### Takeaway
Big coin and gold sums come from competitive or streak events that split a fixed pot:
- Royal Match Lava Quest: 10,000 coins split among the winners.
- Candy Crush Candy Royale: 500 gold split.
- Toon Blast Team Tournament: team pot divided by 50.

Win-streak features (Butler's Gift, Crown Rush, Candy Necklace) pay boosters, not currency.

### Cited Findings
**Royal Match**
- **Lava Quest**: beat 7 consecutive levels on the first try within 24 hours, against 99 other players. "The grand prize of 10.000 coins is shared equally between the winners." Level 66+; retry allowed after 30 minutes. — [Dream Games Help: Lava Quest](https://dreamgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/3-royal-match/faq/187-lava-quest/)
- **Team Battle**: 20 teams compete; the top 5 teams are rewarded, every member gets the same reward, and the top 3 contributors get extra. Hard, Super Hard and Extremely Hard levels give extra shields. — [Dream Games Help: Team Battle](https://dreamgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/3-royal-match/faq/109-team-battle/)
- Winning a Team Battle means "a massive **6000 coin** payout for each member" (player blog, Jan 2024). — [Old Cynic](https://oldcynic.com/royal-match-tips-and-tricks-cheats-for-new-players)
- **King's Cup**: starting it gives **30 minutes of unlimited lives**; 50 participants; ranks 1–10 rewarded. — [Royal Match Fandom: King's Cup](https://royalmatch.fandom.com/wiki/%F0%9F%8F%86_King%27s_Cup)
- **Royal Pass**: a monthly 30-step pass; keys come from wins, with extra keys on Hard, Super Hard and Extremely Hard levels. Details in section 8. — [Dream Games Help: Royal Pass](https://dreamgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/3-royal-match/faq/112-royal-pass/)
- Other coin-paying events (official list): Propeller Madness, Book of Treasure, Team Treasure, Team Tournament, Dragon Nest, Train Journey, Ocean Odyssey, Puzzle Break, Magic Cauldron, Mission Control, Mission Pursuit, Archery Arena, Balloon Rise, Pinata Party, Hidden Temple, Royal League. — [Dream Games Help](https://dreamgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/3-royal-match/faq/10-how-to-get-free-coins/)
- Note: "King's Crown" does not appear as an event name in the official Royal Match help center. The event list includes King's Cup, Royal League, King's Tales, Royal Cup and others. — [Dream Games Help: Events section](https://dreamgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/3-royal-match/section/23-events-and-tournaments/)

**Candy Crush Saga**
- **Episode Race** (race to finish an episode):
  - Normal race payouts: 1st **15/20/25 gold** (rising with win streak), 2nd 8, 3rd 5, 4th 3, 5th 2.
  - Hard or Champions race: 1st **20/30/40 gold**, and Champions Race 1st **45**. Lower placements get 5 and 1.
  - Some tier tables show 25–40 gold plus booster passes.
  - The wiki calls it "the most abundant way of obtaining Gold outside real-world microtransactions".
  - Source: [Candy Crush Fandom: Episode Race](https://candycrush.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_Race)
- **Candy Royale**: a group of 100 players must complete 8 levels on the first attempt within 24 hours. **500 gold bars** are split evenly among the finishers. — [Candy Crush Fandom: Candy Royale](https://candycrush.fandom.com/wiki/Candy_Royale). A search snippet (King Zendesk, which I could not open) says 7 levels; the two sources disagree on the level count.
- **Chocolate Box** (Deluxe tiers Bronze, Silver, Gold): completing the Gold box gives **10 gold** plus a short Colour Bomb pass and 1 hour of Live Forever. Gold can be earned this way about once every 4 days. — [Candy Crush Fandom: Chocolate Box](https://candycrush.fandom.com/wiki/Chocolate_Box)
- **Candy Necklace** (win streak): a first-try streak gives stacked starting boosters, and losing a level resets it. In later updates the game may offer to keep the max bag for 25 gold after a loss. — [Candy Crush Fandom: Candy Necklace](https://candycrush.fandom.com/wiki/Candy_Necklace)
- **Sugar Track / Sugar Drops** were discontinued in March 2020. — [Candy Crush Fandom: Gold](https://candycrush.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_Bar)

**Toon Blast**
- **Crown Rush** (win streak) gives starting boosters:
  - 1 win: 1 Rocket + 1 Bomb
  - 2 wins: 2 Rockets + 2 Bombs
  - 3 wins: 3 Rockets + 3 Bombs
  - Resets on a loss; level 25+.
  - Source: [Peak Help: Crown Rush](https://peakgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/4-toon-blast/faq/24-what-is-crown-rush/)
- **Star Tournament**: 50 players; top ranks rewarded. Wiki amounts: 1st **1,000**, 2nd **500**, 3rd **250 coins**. — [Peak Help](https://peakgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/4-toon-blast/faq/181-what-is-the-star-tournament/); amounts from [Toon Blast Fandom: Star Tournament](https://toonblast.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Tournament)
- **Team Tournament**: 10 teams; the top 5 are rewarded and the shown prize is divided by 50 (official). — [Peak Help: Team Tournament](https://peakgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/4-toon-blast/faq/192-what-is-the-team-tournament/)
  - Wiki amounts: 50,000 / 25,000 / 15,000 / 10,000 / 5,000 coins per team, which is **1,000 / 500 / 300 / 200 / 100 per member**. — [Toon Blast Fandom: Team Tournament](https://toonblast.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Tournament)

### Inferences
- The shared-pot design (fixed pot ÷ number of winners) caps currency inflation no matter how many players succeed. This is worth copying for Pop Archer event rewards.
- Event payouts are 1–2 orders of magnitude larger than per-level pay:
  - Royal Match: 6,000 coins (team win) vs 10 coins (normal level).
  - Candy Crush: about 25 gold (Episode Race) vs 0 gold (level).

### Gaps
- Official reward tables for Royal Match events are images on the wiki and could not be extracted.
- The Toon Blast tournament amounts come from the fan wiki and may be outdated.

## 4. Free chests / star chests: contents

### Takeaway
Chests pay small mixed bundles (coins, boosters, timed unlimited lives, card packs). Exact coin amounts are rarely documented.

### Cited Findings
- **Royal Match** area chest: coins, boosters and cards. — [Dream Games Help](https://dreamgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/3-royal-match/faq/21-how-can-i-obtain-and-use-coins/); [esports.net](https://www.esports.net/news/mobile-games/royal-match-free-coins/)
- **Royal Match** reward containers (analysis of the Dec 2024 Winter Pass):
  - Gift boxes: Orange (1 reward) or Blue (2 rewards).
  - Chests: Normal (2–5 modest rewards) or Premium.
  - Card packs: Brown 2 cards, Green 3, Blue 4, Purple 6 (3+ stars guaranteed), Red 6.
  - Source: [GameMakers, Jan 2025](https://www.gamemakers.com/p/royal-matchs-219-battle-pass-secret)
- **Royal Match** Moves Chest (retired feature): collect 100 extra moves to open a chest of coins and boosters. — [Royal Match Fandom: Moves Chest](https://royalmatch.fandom.com/wiki/Moves_Chest)
- **Toon Blast** star chest (2017 data):
  - Stars from levels fill a chest that opens at 20 stars and gives "meagre rewards".
  - The reviewer notes a set of 3 bombs costs 150 coins, which would take "over 50 levels" of star chests to pay for.
  - Source: [Grave Noise review, Feb 2017 (outdated)](https://www.gravenoisemedia.com/game-review-updated-toon-blast-mobile-free-to-play/)
- **Toon Blast** card packs:
  - Green 2 cards, Blue 3, Purple 4, Red 6, Gold 6 with at least 1 gold card.
  - Earned from events, **Super Hard levels** and Toon Pass.
  - Source: [Peak Help: What are Packs?](https://peakgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/4-toon-blast/faq/615-what-are-packs/)
- **Toon Blast** Toon Pass chests (wiki): e.g. stage 5 free chest = 10 coins + 1 Rocket; paid stage 10 chest = 20 coins + 2 Bombs + 2 Disco Balls + 2 Boxing Gloves. The small coin figures (10–50) seem low compared with the 100-coin continue price and may be transcription errors. — [Toon Blast Fandom: Toon Pass](https://toonblast.fandom.com/wiki/Toon_Pass)

### Inferences
- The chest pattern is cosmetic packaging around 2–5 items, with rewards leaning towards boosters and timed passes rather than currency. This protects the currency-to-real-money exchange rate.

### Gaps
- No verified coin amount for the Royal Match area chest or a current Toon Blast star chest.

## 5. Rewarded video ads

### Takeaway
- **Royal Match: no ads at all.** It carries no "Contains ads" label on Google Play, and analysts describe it as IAP-only.
- **Candy Crush: yes, limited daily rewarded ads.** They give +2 then +1 moves then a UFO after failing, pre-level boosters, occasional lives, and boosters through "Sweet Cinema".
- **Toon Blast: carries the "Contains ads" label.** I found no documentation of what ads reward.

### Cited Findings
- Google Play FR (fetched 2026-09-30) shows "Contient des annonces" (Contains ads) on the Candy Crush Saga and Toon Blast pages, and not on the Royal Match page. — [Candy Crush Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.king.candycrushsaga&hl=fr&gl=FR); [Toon Blast Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=net.peakgames.toonblast&hl=fr&gl=FR); [Royal Match Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.dreamgames.royalmatch&hl=fr&gl=FR)
- "Royal Match doesn't use a single Ad"; it makes money through IAP only. — [esports.net: How does Royal Match make money](https://www.esports.net/news/mobile-games/how-does-royal-match-make-money/) (secondary, via search result); consistent with [Udonis, Sep 2025](https://www.blog.udonis.co/mobile-marketing/mobile-games/royal-match-analysis)
- Candy Crush continue by ad: the first ad gives **+2 moves**, the second **+1 move** (3 total), and the third a free **UFO** activation. The daily supply of these ads is finite, and they are only offered before gold is spent. — [Candy Crush Fandom: Extra Moves](https://candycrush.fandom.com/wiki/Extra_Moves)
- Candy Crush ads also:
  - add a special candy or extra moves before a level;
  - through "Sweet Cinema", give one each of Lollipop Hammer, Free Switch, Colour Bomb, Striped & Wrapped and Jelly Fish **every nine views**;
  - through Ads Quest, give a 10-minute UFO.
  - King staff still called ads a "test feature" in 2021.
  - Sources: [Candy Crush Fandom: Advertisements](https://candycrush.fandom.com/wiki/Advertisements); [Candy Crush Fandom: Gold](https://candycrush.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_Bar)
- Candy Crush lives via ads: players "might be given an opportunity to watch a short video to get one extra life back… usually around **five** advertisements per day; you may have as much as fifteen or none." — [Candy Crush Fandom: Lives](https://candycrush.fandom.com/wiki/Lives)
- King Community threads show ad availability varies by player (some have no ad option, only gold), and mention ads to collect gold after winning Episode Race. — [King Community: no option to watch ads](https://community.king.com/en/candy-crush-saga/discussion/417122/no-option-to-watch-ads-for-more-moves-or-gold-bars); [King Community: gold bars by watching adverts](https://community.king.com/en/candy-crush-saga/discussion/475415/how-can-i-obtain-gold-bars-by-watching-adverts). These are titles and snippets only; the pages returned 403 to direct fetch.

### Inferences
- Candy Crush's "ad ladder" (ad 1: +2 moves, ad 2: +1 move, ad 3: power-up, then paid continue) is a directly transferable pattern for Pop Archer's continue flow. Ads soften the first failure and payment covers the later ones.
- Royal Match proves a top-grossing saga game can skip ads entirely. That depends on a very strong IAP funnel, which a small arcade game will not have.

### Gaps
- What Toon Blast's ads are used for (lives, moves, double rewards?) and their daily caps.
- Whether Candy Crush has a paid ad-removal option. None was found.

## 6. Prices in soft currency (boosters, +5 moves / continue, lives refill, unlimited lives)

### Takeaway
Continue prices, in each game's own currency:
- Royal Match: **900 coins** (about $1.79 at the $1.99 per 1,000 rate).
- Candy Crush: **10 gold** rising to 39 (the first continue is about €1 at the smallest pack rate).
- Toon Blast: **100 coins**, then 200, then 300 on the same level.

Full life refill: Royal Match **900 coins**, Candy Crush **12 gold** (wiki), Toon Blast **100 coins** (wiki and a 2017 review).

### Cited Findings
**Royal Match**
- **+5 moves = 900 coins.** — [Deconstructor of Fun, 2021](https://www.deconstructoroffun.com/blog/2021/3/21/royal-match-the-new-king-from-turkey); still quoted in 2025–26 at [u7buy guide](https://www.u7buy.com/blog/8-insane-tricks-to-farm-coins-in-royal-match/)
- I found no source on whether repeat continues on the same level cost more.
- **Full life refill = 900 coins.** — [Royal Match Fandom: Lives](https://royalmatch.fandom.com/wiki/Lives); [GameMakers, Jan 2025](https://www.gamemakers.com/p/royal-matchs-219-battle-pass-secret), which uses 900 coins / 5 lives = 180 coins per life
- **Creating a team = 100 coins** (official). — [Dream Games Help](https://dreamgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/3-royal-match/faq/54-how-can-i-create-a-new-team/)
- Booster prices: a search snippet claims "3 Rocket boosters for 900 coins", i.e. 300 per booster. **Unverified**: I could not tie it to a fetchable page.
- Coins buy "extra lives, extra moves, boosters" (official). — [Dream Games Help](https://dreamgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/3-royal-match/faq/21-how-can-i-obtain-and-use-coins/)
- No cosmetics are sold for coins; the Royal Pass gives a golden frame and name. — [Dream Games Help: Royal Pass](https://dreamgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/3-royal-match/faq/112-royal-pass/)

**Candy Crush Saga (gold bars)**

## 7. Lives system (max lives, regen time)

### Takeaway
All three use 5 lives with **30 minutes per life** (2.5 hours to full). The paid pass raises the cap to 8 in Royal Match and Toon Blast Golden, and to 10 with Toon Blast Diamond. Teams supply lives socially.

### Cited Findings
- **Royal Match**:
  - Max 5 lives (8 with Royal Pass); 1 life per 30 minutes (official, updated about 2 days before 2026-09-30).
  - Lives can be requested from the team, but not while more than 10 free lives are held.
  - Source: [Dream Games Help: Lives](https://dreamgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/3-royal-match/faq/5-lives/)
  - Unlimited lives are an event/pass reward; the timer runs even when not playing. — [Royal Match Fandom: Lives](https://royalmatch.fandom.com/wiki/Lives)
- **Candy Crush Saga**:
  - Up to 5 lives; 1 life every 30 minutes (max 2.5 hours).
  - Friends can send lives: since Sept 2022 the inbox holds max 200, with max 20 claimed per day.
  - Ads can give a life (section 5).
  - Source: [Candy Crush Fandom: Lives](https://candycrush.fandom.com/wiki/Lives)
- **Toon Blast**:
  - 5 lives; "a free life every 30 minutes" (official, updated about 75 days before 2026-09-30).
  - Teammates can send up to 5 free lives per request.
  - Source: [Peak Help: Lives](https://peakgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/4-toon-blast/faq/6-what-are-lives-hearts-and-how-do-i-get-them/)
  - The fan wiki says 20 minutes per life and a gift box that holds max 10 lives; **this contradicts the official 30 minutes**. — [Toon Blast Fandom: Lives](https://toonblast.fandom.com/wiki/Lives)
  - Team life requests can be made every 4 hours. — [PocketGamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/toon-blast/toon-blast-how-to-get-free-lives)
  - Toon Pass Golden raises max lives to 8, Diamond to 10. — [Peak Help: Toon Pass](https://peakgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/4-toon-blast/faq/322-what-is-toon-pass/)

### Inferences
- "5 lives, 30 minutes" is the genre standard, and "+3 max lives" is the standard pass perk.

### Gaps
- None material.
