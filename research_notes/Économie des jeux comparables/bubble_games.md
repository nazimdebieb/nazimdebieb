# Économies de monnaie des jeux mobiles à bulles (Bubble Witch 3 Saga, Bubble Shooter d'Ilyon, Bubble Pop!, Pang Adventures, Bubble Trouble)

Collected 2026-09-30. Store price lists were pulled live on that date from the French App Store (EUR, `apps.apple.com/fr/...`) and French Google Play (EUR). USD figures come from apppricinglab.com snapshots dated March–June 2026. Wiki and community data on in-game coin amounts is mostly **old (2017–2021)**, and each such item is flagged. Important correction to the brief: **"Bubble Pop! Puzzle Game Legend" is made by BitMango, Inc., not Ilyon.** Ilyon's flagship is "Bubble Shooter" (iOS "Bubble Shooter: Pop & Blast" id604044220, Android `bubbleshooter.orig`). Both are covered below.

## Q1 — Coins earned per level, star/score bonuses, end-of-world rewards

### Takeaway
None of these games publishes a fixed "coins per level" figure. Bubble Witch 3 doesn't really pay hard currency (gold bars) per level: stars go into a **Piggy Bank** that you have to *buy* to open (2,99 €), and it holds about 32 gold bars. In Ilyon's Bubble Shooter, players say coins per level are tiny ("several levels for ONE coin"). Its main free sources are daily bonus, videos, map rewards, and a reward every 10 levels.

### Cited Findings
**Bubble Witch 3 Saga (King)**
- Piggy Bank: you fill it by earning stars when you beat a level. "The higher your score… the more stars… the more Gold Bars will be added to your Piggy Bank! Especially on those 'Hard' and 'Super Hard' levels." You pay to break it once it says "Piggy Bank available", or keep going until "Piggy Bank full" — [BW3 Fandom wiki, Piggy Bank (via MediaWiki API)](https://bubblewitch3.fandom.com/wiki/Piggy_Bank)
- The Piggy Bank is sold as an IAP for **2,99 €** (FR App Store, 2026-09-30) — [App Store FR](https://apps.apple.com/fr/app/id1095254858); **$2.99** US (June 2026 snapshot) — [AppPricingLab](https://apppricinglab.com/app/apple/1095254858)
- The Piggy Bank is full at "something like 32 gold bars". This comes from a player Q&A via a search snippet; the page itself returned 403, so it is **unverified and undated** — [AppGamer Q&A](https://www.appgamer.com/bubble-witch-3-saga/answers/4848-what-do-i-do-with-the-piggy-bank-go)
- Levels also give **Star Dust**, a meta currency used to rebuild and decorate Stella's house. Store description: "trouver la poussière d'étoiles dont Stella a besoin pour reconstruire, aménager et personnaliser son nouveau logis" — [Google Play FR](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.king.bubblewitch3&hl=fr&gl=FR)
- Latest version 10.19.0.0 (22 Sept 2026) adds "nouvelles ligues et des défis à durée limitée" that give rewards — [App Store FR](https://apps.apple.com/fr/app/id1095254858)

**Bubble Shooter (Ilyon Dynamics)**
- Coins are the game's currency. You get them free from the daily bonus, by watching a video, or from special rewards on the map. "Each level of 10 levels has a level that when you complete, you will receive a corresponding reward". This comes from the Ilyon help center via a search snippet; the page now redirects to Miniclip support and the article no longer exists (checked 2026-09-30) — [Ilyon help: What are coins used for?](https://bubbleshooterandroid.ilyon.net/hc/en-us/articles/360015981199-What-are-coins-used-for)
- A user review complains: "Complete several levels for ONE coin? Come on." (undated) — [Game Solver review aggregate](https://game-solver.com/bubble-shooter-pop-bubbles-2/)
- The store description says: "atteignez l'objectif de chaque niveau, nettoyez le tableau et collectez les pièces", "compléter les missions et gagner des pièces et des récompenses", and gives the level count as **6 500+** — [App Store FR](https://apps.apple.com/fr/app/id604044220)
- Free in-level power-ups not bought with coins: pop 7 bubbles in a row to get a **fireball**, or drop 10+ bubbles in one shot to get a **bomb** — [App Store FR](https://apps.apple.com/fr/app/id604044220)
- The store also mentions a quest path that unlocks boosters: "suivez le chemin des quêtes pour débloquer de nouveaux boosters" — [Google Play FR](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=bubbleshooter.orig&hl=fr&gl=FR)

**Bubble Pop! Puzzle Game Legend (BitMango)**
- "Clear the board and win coins". Daily login streaks give power-ups, and an accumulated "level bar" unlocks rewards — [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bubble-pop-puzzle-game-legend/id1444990764)

### Inferences
- In the King model, stars and score feed a paid Piggy Bank instead of paying out directly. The player "earns" hard currency but has to pay 2,99 € to cash it. For Pop Archer this suggests a lever: pay a small amount of coins per level and use a piggy bank as a monetisation bridge.
- Ilyon's small per-level payouts plus coin-priced continues push players toward rewarded video and IAP.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable figure for coins per level (normal vs. hard vs. boss) in Ilyon Bubble Shooter or BitMango Bubble Pop. The Ilyon help articles are gone, and Fandom/King community pages returned 402/403.
- I found no end-of-chapter reward amounts for BW3 from 2024–2026.

## Q2 — Daily login gift / spin, daily challenges & events, free chests

### Takeaway
BW3's daily gift (2017 wiki data) is a 5-day cycle: 50 → 150 Star Dust, then boosters and 30 min of unlimited lives, with **no gold bars**. Its events (Magic/Dragon Challenge) are 7-day quest ladders with Bronze/Silver/Gold tiers at 7/11/14 quests. Ilyon and BitMango advertise daily rewards and daily tasks but publish no amounts.

### Cited Findings
- BW3 Daily Gift (released 30 May 2017, mobile only), 5-day round: Day 1 50 Star Dust · Day 2 150 Star Dust · Day 3 1× Stereo Bolt booster · Day 4 30 min unlimited lives · Day 5 1× Sparkling Blast booster (**2017 data**) — [BW3 Fandom, Daily Gift](https://bubblewitch3.fandom.com/wiki/Daily_Gift)
- BW3 Magic Challenge / Dragon Challenge (2017): "Complete Quests in 7 days… 14 Quests for Gold, 11 Quests for Silver, and 7 Quests for Bronze" (**2017**) — [BW3 Fandom, Dragon Challenge](https://bubblewitch3.fandom.com/wiki/Dragon_Challenge)
- BW3 in 2026: new leagues and limited-time challenges that give rewards (v10.19.0.0, 22 Sept 2026) — [App Store FR](https://apps.apple.com/fr/app/id1095254858)
- BW3: you can visit friends' houses to get rewards ("visitez celles de vos amis pour obtenir des récompenses") — [Google Play FR](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.king.bubblewitch3&hl=fr&gl=FR)
- Ilyon Bubble Shooter: "Collectez d'incroyables récompenses quotidiennes", with regular events and a quest path — [App Store FR](https://apps.apple.com/fr/app/id604044220); [Google Play FR](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=bubbleshooter.orig&hl=fr&gl=FR)
- Ilyon: users mention a collectible card system where completing a set gives a "bunch of stuff" (App Store review summary, undated) — [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id604044220)
- BitMango Bubble Pop: daily login streak prizes, daily tasks, tournaments/leaderboard events — [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bubble-pop-puzzle-game-legend/id1444990764)

### Inferences
- Short 5-day login cycles that mostly give boosters, lives, and a meta currency seem to be the King norm. They avoid giving much hard currency for free.

### Gaps
- No 2024–2026 daily-gift amounts for any of the games. No daily spin found for BW3, and I found nothing on Ilyon's daily spin amounts or on free chests.

## Q3 — Rewarded video ads

### Takeaway
BW3 has ads (the Play Store flags "Contient des annonces"). According to a King community snippet, a rewarded ad gives **+1 gold bar**, replacing an earlier "Free Helper". Ilyon uses rewarded video for **+3 extra bubbles** and for free coins. BitMango uses it for extra moves and boosts.

### Cited Findings
- BW3: "After Watch Ad, you get Gold Bar +1. Free Gold Bar is instead of Free Helper (new feature since early February)". This is from a King community thread via search snippet; the page returned 403 and the **year is unknown** — [King Community: Free Gold Bar in BW3??](https://community.king.com/en/bubble-witch-saga/discussion/491719/free-gold-bar-in-bw3)
- BW3 Google Play FR listing shows "Contient des annonces" (2026-09-30) — [Google Play FR](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.king.bubblewitch3&hl=fr&gl=FR)
- Ilyon: you can get free coins "by watching a video" (help center, now removed) — [Ilyon help](https://bubbleshooterandroid.ilyon.net/hc/en-us/articles/360015981199-What-are-coins-used-for)
- Ilyon: video rewards give "three extra bubbles"; users report ads "after every level" (user reviews, undated) — [Game Solver](https://game-solver.com/bubble-shooter-pop-bubbles-2/)
- BitMango Bubble Pop: optional rewarded videos for additional moves and boosts — [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bubble-pop-puzzle-game-legend/id1444990764)
- Bubble Trouble 3 (One up games, by original author Kresimir Cvitanovic) has a REVIVE on the Game Over screen; a timer was removed in a 2026 update. Reviewers of Bubble Trouble Classic complain "5 minutes de jeux pour 15 minutes de pubs" (review 25/03/2025) — [App Store FR, Bubble Trouble 3](https://apps.apple.com/fr/app/id1643029539); [App Store FR, Bubble Trouble Classic](https://apps.apple.com/fr/app/id1453111037)

### Inferences
- A rewarded-ad continue worth "+3 bubbles" (Ilyon) against a paid continue of "+5 bubbles" (BW3, 9 gold bars) is a useful benchmark: an ad continue is worth roughly 60% of a paid continue.

### Gaps
- No reliable data on ad frequency caps (ads per day) for any title.

## Q4 — Prices in soft/hard currency (boosters, continues, lives)

### Takeaway
BW3 (gold bars): a **+5 bubbles continue costs 9 gold bars**, a **full lives refill costs 12 gold bars**, and pre-level boosters cost **about 6–10 gold bars** (wiki data, likely 2017–2019). Ilyon spends coins on extra moves after failing and on boosters, but publishes no coin prices.

### Cited Findings
- BW3 boosters/spells: the first ones are free, after that "they will cost from 6 gold bars to probably around 10 gold bars". Released boosters are Stereo Bolt, Sparkling Blast, Magic Beam, +5 Bubble, Shooting Star (level 73), and Rainbow Rush (level 93) (**older wiki data, undated**) — [BW3 Fandom, Boosters](https://bubblewitch3.fandom.com/wiki/Boosters)
- BW3 continue: when you fail, "Play on for 9 Gold Bars" gives 5 extra bubbles (**undated wiki**) — [BW3 Fandom, +5 Bubble](https://bubblewitch3.fandom.com/wiki/%2B5_Bubble)
- BW3 lives refill: "It costs 12 gold bars to refill your lives" (**undated wiki**) — [BW3 Fandom, Lives](https://bubblewitch3.fandom.com/wiki/Lives)
- Ilyon: coins are spent on extra moves when you fail a level and on boosters/"jolts" (pre-game boosters, which can be won or bought) — [Ilyon help](https://bubbleshooterandroid.ilyon.net/hc/en-us/articles/360015981199-What-are-coins-used-for); [Ilyon help: Jolts](https://bubbleshooterandroid.ilyon.net/hc/en-us/articles/360018093720-Jolts)
- Double Bubble Trouble (TLBM, an unofficial Pang/Bubble Trouble clone) prices items directly in real money: "3 Extra Hearts for this Game Round" 0,99 €, 5 Hearts 1,99 €, 100 Bombs 0,99 €, 1 000 Bombs 2,99 €, 10 000 Bombs 9,99 € — [App Store FR](https://apps.apple.com/fr/app/id632008001)

### Inferences
- The BW3 ratios are continue 9 : refill 12 : booster 6–10. A lives refill costs about 1.3 continues, which gives a template for Pop Archer's coin pricing.

### Gaps
- No verified 2024–2026 in-game gold-bar prices for BW3, and no coin prices for Ilyon or BitMango boosters or continues. Cosmetics: BW3 has house decoration paid with Star Dust, but I found no prices.

## Q5 — Lives system

### Takeaway
BW3: **5 lives max, 1 life per 30 min**, and a refill costs 12 gold bars. Friends can gift lives. Bubble Struggle: Adventures switched to an **energy** system in 2023.

### Cited Findings
- BW3: max 5 lives; "One life is refilled every 30 minutes"; you lose a life on fail or quit; lives come from friends, waiting, buying, or claiming an offer — [BW3 Fandom, Lives](https://bubblewitch3.fandom.com/wiki/Lives)
- BW3 daily gift includes "30 min Unlimited Lives" (2017) — [BW3 Fandom, Daily Gift](https://bubblewitch3.fandom.com/wiki/Daily_Gift)
- Bubble Struggle: Adventures v1.88 (22/09/2023): "Removed paywall, all levels are available to all players. Playing uses energy." — [App Store FR](https://apps.apple.com/fr/app/id947676130)

### Gaps
- No lives or regen data found for Ilyon Bubble Shooter or BitMango Bubble Pop. The Ilyon description emphasises offline play, and I couldn't confirm a lives cap.

## Q6 — Real-money offers (packs, starter pack, remove ads, VIP/pass, premium price)

### Takeaway
BW3 sells **bundled "Offers" from 0,99 € to 22,99 €**, a **Piggy Bank at 2,99 €**, and a **Royal Pass at 5,99 €**. Google Play's range goes up to 119,99 €. Ilyon sells **coin packs from 0,99 € to 14,99 €, bundles up to 39,99 €, and a Beginner's Bundle at 1,99 €**, plus a weekly VIP and a remove-ads option ($4.99 per a user review). **Pang Adventures is premium (3,99 € on Google Play), with no IAP and no ads.** It currently returns 404 on the App Store in FR/US/DE/RU.

Modes: Tour (100+ levels, bosses), Score Attack (3 lives, 0 continues), Panic (99 levels) — [Google Play FR](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.dotemu.pangadventures&hl=fr&gl=FR)

The US prices of $1.99 / $3.99 / $5.99 / $9.99 appeared in search results for the same app.

### Inferences
- Price-point benchmarks for Pop Archer: an entry offer at 0,99–1,99 €, a starter/beginner bundle at 1,99 €, a piggy bank at 2,99 €, a pass at 5,99 € (both King's Royal Pass and BitMango's Premium Pass), remove ads at about 5–6 € (5,99 € for Bubble Trouble, $4.99 for Ilyon), and a top bundle at 20–40 €.
- Bubble Struggle's coin ladder (250 c = 1,99 € up to 3 000 c = 9,99 €) implies about 125 c/€ at the low end and 300 c/€ at the top, a 2.4× bulk bonus. It is the closest Pang-like soft-currency reference found.

### Gaps
- Gold-bar and coin quantities inside each BW3, Ilyon and BitMango pack are not shown on store pages. The only data point is a review claiming BW3 charges "2.99 to get 45" gold bars, seen as a search snippet, so it is unverified and undated.
- No price was found for Ilyon's VIP. I found no Deconstructor of Fun or GameRefinery teardown of these specific titles.
