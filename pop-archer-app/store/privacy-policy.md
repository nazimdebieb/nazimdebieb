# Pop Archer — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: to be dated on publication_

Pop Archer ("the game") is developed by an independent developer (contact: your email).

## Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device only: levels, stars and scores, coins, unlocked outfits and arrows, boosts, sound setting, and the dates of your daily gift and free chests. This data never leaves your device, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

## Data we collect

The current version of the game collects no personal data and sends nothing over the internet. It has no account system, no analytics and no tracking.

## Advertising and purchases

The current version does not show real ads and has no real payments. If a future version adds ads (Google AdMob) or in-app purchases (Google Play Billing), this policy will be updated before release to explain what data those Google services process, and you will be asked for consent where the law requires it.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.
