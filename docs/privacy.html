Privacy policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Pop Archer (“the game”) is an arcade game for Android made by an independent developer. This policy explains what happens to your data when you play.

In short

The game collects no personal data and sends nothing over the internet. Your progress stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

To remember your game, Pop Archer saves the following on your device only:

  • levels completed, stars and Survival scores;
  • coins, unlocked outfits and arrow effects, and boosts;
  • your settings (sound effects, music, vibration);
  • the dates of your daily gift and free chests, and whether you have seen the tutorial.

This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset progress in the game, by clearing the app's data, or by uninstalling the game.

Data we collect

None. The game has no account system, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising identifier. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Permissions

  • Vibration: short vibrations when you pop bubbles or get hit. You can turn them off in the game's settings.
  • Internet: included by the app framework. The current version makes no network requests.

Advertising and in-app purchases

The current version shows no real ads and takes no real payments. If a future version adds ads (Google AdMob) or in-app purchases (Google Play Billing), this policy will be updated before that version is released to explain what data those Google services process, and you will be asked for consent where the law requires it.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

For any question about this policy or your data: contact email to be added.
