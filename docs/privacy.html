Privacy policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Pop Archer (“the game”) is an arcade game for Android made by Vestolia, an independent game studio. This policy explains what happens to your data when you play.

In short

Your progress stays on your device. Vestolia collects no personal data and has no server. The game shows ads from Google AdMob and sells optional items through Google Play, and these Google services process some data as described below.

Data stored on your device

To remember your game, Pop Archer saves the following on your device only:

  • levels completed, stars and Survival scores;
  • coins, unlocked outfits, arrow effects, weapons and boosts;
  • your settings (sound effects, music, vibration, controls, language);
  • the dates of your daily gift and free chests, and whether you have seen the tutorial.

This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset progress in the game, by clearing the app's data, or by uninstalling the game.

Data we collect

None. Vestolia has no account system, no analytics and no server, and receives none of your data. The game does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Permissions

  • Vibration: short vibrations when you pop bubbles or get hit. You can turn them off in the game's settings.
  • Internet and network state: to load ads and to talk to Google Play for purchases. The game itself plays offline.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): used by Google AdMob, see below. You can reset or delete it in your phone's settings (Google → Ads).
  • Billing: lets Google Play handle in-app purchases.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ads never play during gameplay. You can choose to watch a short video ad for a reward (for example to continue or to double your coins), and a short ad may sometimes show when you leave a results screen. Buying “No ads” removes the ads you don't choose.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with the ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time with Settings → Privacy choices. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Optional items (coin packs, the season pass, “No ads”, the armory and starter packs, the piggy bank) are sold through Google Play Billing. Payment is handled entirely by Google: Vestolia never sees your payment details. The game only receives a confirmation of the purchase, used on your device to give you the item and, for one-time items, to restore it if you reinstall the game. See Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. Vestolia does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
