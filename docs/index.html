Aim your bow and shoot straight up: every bubble you hit splits in two, then in two again, until the smallest ones pop. 200+ levels across themed worlds, epic bosses, special bubbles and an endless Survival mode.

Features

  • 200+ levels on a winding map: forest, desert ruins, deep sea, outer space, frozen peaks, volcano, sky kingdom, haunted castle, jungle temple, trap castle… and new worlds on the way
  • A new daily challenge every day, and 30 trophies to unlock
  • A boss at the end of every world
  • 7 special bubbles, from golden to the dreaded black bubble
  • Endless Survival mode with combos
  • Outfits and arrow effects to unlock
  • Plays offline, with no ads during gameplay
  • Weapons to unlock in the armory: wind arrow, ballista and more
