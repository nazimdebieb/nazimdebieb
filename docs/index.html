Aim your bow and shoot straight up: every bubble you hit splits in two, then in two again, until the smallest ones pop. 48 levels across 4 worlds, epic bosses, special bubbles and an endless Survival mode.

Features

  • 48 levels in 4 worlds: forest, desert ruins, deep sea and outer space
  • A boss at the end of every world
  • 7 special bubbles, from golden to the dreaded black bubble
  • Endless Survival mode with combos
  • Outfits and arrow effects to unlock
  • Plays offline, with no ads during gameplay
